FriendoTube privacy policy

Last updated October 22, 2026.

FriendoTube is a browser extension that shares the videos YouTube recommends you with the friend groups you join, and shows you theirs. This page explains exactly what it collects and who can see it.

What we collect

  • The name you choose when you start. No email, password or Google account is asked for. The extension stores a random access key in your browser to identify you.
  • Videos YouTube recommends you on the YouTube home page and in the sidebar next to a video: the video ID, title, channel name, and when it was seen. You can turn this off at any time in the extension popup ("Share what YouTube recommends me with my groups").
  • Videos you choose to recommend, with the note you write.
  • Which groups you are in.

We do not collect your watch history, searches, comments, subscriptions, YouTube or Google account details, cookies, or anything from any website other than youtube.com.

Who can see it

Only the other members of the groups you join see your name, your feed videos and your recommendations. Groups can only be joined with an invite code. Your data is never sold, never used for advertising, and never shared with anyone else.

How long we keep it

Feed videos and recommendations are deleted automatically after 14 days. Your name and group memberships stay until you delete them.

Deleting your data

Open the extension popup and click Delete my account + data. That removes your name, feed videos, recommendations and memberships from the server immediately. Leaving a group removes you from it straight away. Uninstalling the extension stops all collection.

Where it is stored

On a server operated by Collin Rijock, reached over HTTPS at friendotube.collinrijock.com.

Chrome Web Store Limited Use

The use of information received from the extension adheres to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data is used only to provide FriendoTube's single purpose: sharing YouTube recommendations within your friend groups.
