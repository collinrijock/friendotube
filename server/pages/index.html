FriendoTube

See what YouTube is recommending your friends, and send each other videos.

  • A From your friends row on your YouTube home: videos your friends sent you, and videos from their feeds that YouTube didn't show you.
  • Recommend any of them, or click Send to friends on any video, with an optional note.
  • Make a group, share its invite code, and you're in.

Get it from the Chrome Web Store (works in Chrome and Copper). Privacy policy.
